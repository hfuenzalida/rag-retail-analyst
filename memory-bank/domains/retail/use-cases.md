# Retail Use Cases (v1)

1. **Weekly revenue variance** — "Why did sales drop last week?"
2. **Stock-out risk** — "Which SKUs are at risk in the next 7 days?"
3. **High margin + stock-outs** — "What products drive revenue but are often out of stock?"
4. **Margin by category** — "Which categories contributed most to margin last quarter?"
5. **Reorder suggestions** — "What should we reorder this week?"

Each answer must include citations to underlying metrics or abstain.
